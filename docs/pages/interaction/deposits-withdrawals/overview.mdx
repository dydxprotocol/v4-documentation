# Deposits and Withdrawals

This guide provides a **step-by-step** explanation of deposit and withdrawal processes on the dYdX Chain. It covers Skip Go (`regular`), direct IBC transfers, and the deprecated Skip Go Fast (`Instant`) and Coinbase-via-Noble deposit methods, along with troubleshooting methods and best practices to ensure seamless transactions.

:::info
Since the v9.9 upgrade, dYdX USDC is backed by USDC on Injective instead of Noble. USDC deposits come from Injective. Noble deposits, including Noble auto-forwarding addresses, are rejected (code 13) and refunded on Noble. Withdrawals go to Noble, until governance closes the Noble exit, or to Injective. See the [Canonical USDC integrator guide](/interaction/deposits-withdrawals/usdc-migration).
:::
